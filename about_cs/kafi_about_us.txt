Welcome to Kafi, your neighborhood coffee shop in the heart of Tadipatri, Andhra Pradesh. At Kafi, we believe coffee is more than just a drink—it’s an experience, a moment of joy, and a way to connect with others.

Our Story
Founded in 2015 by Vignesh, Kafi started with a simple mission: to bring high-quality, freshly brewed coffee to the community. Inspired by South India's rich coffee heritage, Vignesh partnered with local farmers in Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh to source the finest beans. Roasted in-house, our coffee reflects the dedication and passion that go into every cup.

Delivery & Locations Served
Enjoy our coffee in the cozy café or have it delivered straight to your home or office. We proudly serve Tadipatri and nearby towns, ensuring you never miss your favorite brew.

Our Menu
From bold espresso blends to refreshing cold brews, artisanal teas, and fresh-baked goods, Kafi offers something for everyone. We also provide plant-based milk options and gluten-free snacks to cater to all dietary preferences.

Community & Sustainability
At Kafi, we are committed to sustainability and community engagement. We use eco-friendly packaging, support local farmers, and host events like live music nights, art showcases, and community gatherings, making Kafi a hub for creativity and connection.
